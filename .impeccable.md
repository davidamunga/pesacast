# Design context

Used by the design skills when working on the project's website (`site/`). Confirmed by the maintainer on 2026-10-25.

- **Audience:** two groups, equally: people who run a shop or office and want payments to show up on a screen (non-technical, often on a budget Android phone), and developers who want to build on the webhook. The home page gives each a clear path: "Set up a phone" and "Build on it".
- **Use cases:** understand in one minute what PesaCast does and does not do; get a phone and computer set up in about 15 minutes; check that it is safe; integrate a server.
- **Tone:** plain and a little dry, in the manner of dumbtv.org. Short sentences, honest about limits, no hype, no exclamation marks. State what it does and, with equal weight, what it does not.
- **Language:** English only for now. Swahili may follow once the English copy is settled and a Swahili speaker can check it.
- **Hosting:** a static folder with no build step (Cloudflare Pages or similar). No JavaScript, no cookies, no analytics, nothing loaded from other websites; a strict Content-Security-Policy in `_headers` enforces it.
- **Visual direction:** "the till slip". Warm paper tones tinted toward the brand green (#16A34A family), ink-dark text, one printed receipt as the memorable element on the home page. Fraunces for headings, Public Sans for text, both self-hosted. Light by default, with a matching dark scheme from `prefers-color-scheme`. No glow, no gradients on text, no icon grids.
