# Design context

Used by the design skills when working on the project's website (`site/`). Confirmed by the maintainer on 2026-10-25.

- **Audience:** two groups, equally: people who run a shop or office and want payments to show up on a screen (non-technical, often on a budget Android phone), and developers who want to build on the webhook. The home page gives each a clear path: "Set up a phone" and "Build on it".
- **Use cases:** understand in one minute what PesaCast does and does not do; get a phone and computer set up in about 15 minutes; check that it is safe; integrate a server.
- **Tone:** plain and a little dry, in the manner of dumbtv.org. Short sentences, honest about limits, no hype, no exclamation marks. State what it does and, with equal weight, what it does not.
- **Language:** English only for now. Swahili may follow once the English copy is settled and a Swahili speaker can check it.
- **Hosting:** a Vite multi-page build (`site/`, output `site/dist`) that ships plain HTML and one stylesheet, hosted on any static host (Vercel is configured in `vercel.json`; Cloudflare Pages and Netlify read `public/_headers`). No JavaScript in the output, no cookies, no analytics, nothing loaded from other websites; a strict Content-Security-Policy (kept identical in `_headers` and `vercel.json`) enforces it, and `site/check.py` checks the build.
- **Visual direction (revised again 2026-10-25: the soft macOS version read as over-designed, "vibecoded"):** a proper, clean, conventional website. Flat colours, one accent (brand green), the system font with a self-hosted Public Sans fallback, 1px borders instead of shadows, 8px corners, plenty of white space and a readable line length. No frosted or blurred surfaces, no gradients, no badges or halos. Pages explain; they do not perform. White theme only (revised 2026-10-26, on request). The one illustration is the home hero: a flat, CSS-only loop of an Android phone receiving an M-PESA text, keeping it, and sending it to your platform, the desktop app and other integrations. It uses no scripts, stops under prefers-reduced-motion, and shows invented sample data.
